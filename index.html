How we work

From first idea to launch, and after

Every product goes through the same four steps, so you always know where it stands.

  1. Step 1

    Understand

    We start with what the product needs to do, who it is for and what a good result looks like.

  2. Step 2

    Design

    We shape the experience and the look before the build starts, so everyone can see where it is going.

  3. Step 3

    Build and test

    We build in short steps and test as we go, so problems are found early, not at the end.

  4. Step 4

    Launch and improve

    We publish, watch how it is used and keep making it better with updates.
